HOW TO OPEN SYLVORIN IN UNREAL 5.8
==================================

You do NOT use "New Project".

EASIEST - double-click this file:
  OPEN-SYLVORIN-PROJECT.bat

OR in File Explorer go to:
  [this folder]\Unreal\Sylvorin.uproject

Double-click Sylvorin.uproject

OR on "My Projects" screen:
  - Look at the BOTTOM LEFT for "Browse" or a folder icon
  - Or click the ... three dots menu

NEVER click "New Project" for Sylvorin.
